# ForeverDB — Valley of Trials: primeros 10 niveles

## Puntos clave
- Orden de misiones 1–5: Kaltunk → *Your Place in the World*; Gornek → *Cutting Teeth*, *Wayward Weapons*, *Sting of the Scorpid*; Galgar → *Galgar's Cactus Apple Surprise*; Foreman Thazz'ril → *Lazy Peons*, *Thazz'ril's Pick*; Hana'zua → *Sarkoth*; Zureetha Fargaze → *Burning Blade Medallion*, *Report to Sen'jin Village*.
- Valley: comida Zlagk (42.7, 67.2), bienes generales Duokna (42.6, 67.3), ropa y reparación Huklah (40.6, 67.9), sanador espiritual 44.2, 69.4.
- Sen'jin Village: Mage trainer Un'Thuwa (56.3, 75.2), comida Hai'zan, bienes K'waii, armas y reparación Trayexir, **Enchanting Pa'zula (56.6, 73.7)**, Herbalism Mishiki, Alchemy Miao'zan.
- Razor Hill: posada Innkeeper Grosk (51.6, 41.7), Cooking Cook Torka, First Aid Rawrk. Sin entrenador de Mage.
- Misiones 6–10 en Sen'jin: Vel'rin Fang, Master Vornal, Master Gadrin (*Report to Orgnil*, *Minshina's Skull*, *Zalazane* a 10), Lar Prowltusk (*Thwarting Kolkar Aggression*), Pa'zula (*A Pain in the Neck*), Xar'Ti (*Bloodtalon Matriarch*).
- Orgrimmar: Mage trainer Uthel'nay (38.9, 85.9), posada Gryshka (54.1, 68.7), vuelo 45.3, 63.7, banco Soran, subasta Wabang (55.6, 64.7), maestro de armas Hanashi (81.5, 19.5).
