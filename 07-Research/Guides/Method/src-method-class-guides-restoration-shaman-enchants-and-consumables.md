# Method: Restoration Shaman, encantamientos y consumibles

## Puntos clave
- Encantamientos: arma Healing Power / 2H Mighty Healing Power; cabeza y piernas Arcanum of Focus; collar, muñecas y guantes Healing Power; capa Subtlety; pecho y botas Mystic Rugged Armor Kit.
- Consumibles: Flask of Distilled Wisdom; Greater Cleric's, Greater Mageblood, Owl, Sages; Major Mender's Potion; Sage's Tea; Brilliant Mana Oil o Brilliant Wizard Oil.
