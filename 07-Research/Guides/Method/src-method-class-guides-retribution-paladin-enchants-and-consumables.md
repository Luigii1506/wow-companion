# Method: Retribution Paladin, encantamientos y consumibles

## Puntos clave
- Tabla doble Deep Ret / Holy Shock: cabeza y piernas Lesser Arcanum of Voracity; collar Strength / Spell Power; capa Minor Agility; pecho Greater Stats; muñecas Superior Strength; guantes Greater Strength / Holy Power; botas Greater Agility; arma Crusader / Spellblasting.
- Frasco: Supreme Power si existe, si no Flask of Natural Precision (+5 % Expertise). Elixires: Grizzly, Greater Arcane, Holy Power, Mongoose, Owl, Sages, Juju Might, Greater Mageblood.
- Brilliant Wizard Oil (acumula con Windfury Totem); Baked Salmon + Dragonbreath Chili; Major Spellblasting (Shock) o Major Frenzy (Deep Ret), Major Mana, Free Action; Dark/Demonic Rune.
