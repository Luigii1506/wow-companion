# Method: Holy Paladin, BiS y stats

## Puntos clave
- Stats: **Healing Power > Intellect > Crit > MP5 > Spirit > Haste**. Crit alimenta Illumination; Spirit rinde al 30 % en combate por Reverence.
- Pre-raid BiS publicado (marcado "Work In Progress"): Living Crown (LW), Glowing Eye of Mordresh (Razorfen Downs), Theramore/Darkspear Mantle (reputación exaltada), Hide of the Wild (LW), Breastplate of Salvation (BS), Dryad's Wrist Bindings (Silverwing/Warsong), Theramore/Raider Handwraps, Belt of the Archmage y Mooncloth Boots (Tailoring), Martyr's Legplate (BS), Warden's Signet of Resolve (The Watchers exaltado), Blessed Band of Light (75 Tarnished Undermine Real), Serenity Field (125 Real), Uther's Strength y Hand of Edward the Odd (BoE), Lei of the Lifegiver (Stormpike/Frostwolf), Libram of Infusion (Enchanting).
- Alternativas más fáciles: Whitesoul Helm, Dawnbringer Shoulders, piezas Prefect's y Sageblade + Evergreen Shield (BS), Orb of the Darkmoon, Soulforge Breastplate/Boots (misión), Libram of Economy (50 Real).
- BiS, tier y trinkets: "Work In Progress".
