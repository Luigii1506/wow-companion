# Method — Feral DPS Druid: encantamientos y consumibles

## Puntos clave
- Encantamientos (aviso: faltan los nuevos): cabeza y piernas Lesser Arcanum of Voracity o of Constitution; collar Agility o Strength; capa Lesser Agility; muñecas Greater Agility o Superior Strength; guantes Superior Strength o Superior Agility; pies Greater Agility; arma Crusader; pecho Forceful Rugged Armor Kit o Greater Stats.
- Los consumibles se acumulan por stat mientras no pisen un buff mejor.
- BiS: comida Steaming Stag Steak o Swiftstrike Steak; Dense Sharpening Stone/Weightstone; Elixir of the Grizzly, Mongoose, Owl, Whale, Fortitude, Major Troll's Blood; Juju Might; Flask of Natural Aggression; Goblin Sapper Charge; Mighty Rage Potion; Manual Crowd Pummeler; runas; Thistle Tea; buffs de Blasted Lands.
- Remite a la guía general de consumibles y encantamientos de Method.
