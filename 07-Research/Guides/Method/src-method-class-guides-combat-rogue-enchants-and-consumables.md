# Method — Combat Rogue: encantamientos y consumibles

## Puntos clave
- Encantamientos: Core Armor Kit (cabeza, piernas), Necklace/Cloak/Bracer/Gloves Agility (Superior en muñecas y guantes), Chest Greater Stats, Boots Minor Speed, Crusader (mano principal), Agility (secundaria); hombros vacío.
- Consumibles: Flask of Natural Accuracy, Elixir of the Mongoose, Major Frenzy Potion, Grilled Squid, Thistle Tea, Deadly + Instant Poison; Dense Sharpening Stone o Dense Weightstone según arma; Heavy Runecloth Bandage.
