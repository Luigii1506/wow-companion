# Method — Balance Druid: encantamientos y consumibles

## Puntos clave
- Encantamientos: 2M Spellblasting (alt. Mighty Spell Power), 1M Spell Power, cabeza y piernas Arcanum of Focus (piernas alt. Mystic Rugged Armor Kit), collar Spell Power, capa Subtlety (alt. Greater Resistance), pecho Mystic Rugged Armor Kit (alt. Greater Stats), muñecas Greater Spellpower, guantes Arcane Power, pies Speed (alt. Mystic Rugged Armor Kit).
- Frasco: Flask of Supreme Power. Elixires ofensivos: Owl, Greater Mageblood, Greater Arcane, Nature Power, Sages/Whale. Defensivos: Greater Fortitude, Major Troll's Blood, Greater Defense.
- Pociones: Major Spellblasting, Elemental Purging, Major Mana, Dragonfire (AoE en melee), Caustic Smog (AoE a distancia), Venomous Blood (nicho); protecciones, Free Action, Living Action, Limited Invulnerability.
- Comida Baked Salmon; aceite Brilliant Wizard Oil; Dark/Demonic Rune; Saf-T Clever Dynamite; Goblin Sapper Charge (Engineering, cuerpo a cuerpo).
