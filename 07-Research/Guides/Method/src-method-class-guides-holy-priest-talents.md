# Method — Holy Priest Talents

## Puntos clave
- **Deep Holy Raid (16/35/0)**, decodificada del widget. Holy: Improved Renew 3, Holy Specialization 5, Divine Fury 5, Holy Nova, Inspiration 3, Holy Reach 2, Improved Healing 3, Holy Binding, Litany of Light 2, Spirit of Redemption, Spiritual Guidance 5, Spiritual Healing 3, Prayer of Mending. Disc: Twin Disciplines 5, Silent Resolve 3, Mental Strength 4, Inner Focus, Meditation 3.
  - Variantes: −2 Mental Strength → Twilight Focus en combates de mucho AoE; −1 Holy Reach → Mental Strength 5/5 si no hace falta alcance.
  - Excluye Improved PW:S y Soul Warding (no quitar ira a los tanques) y Mental Agility (en raid se castea, no se spamean instantáneos).
- **Disc/Holy Penance & Soul Warding (21/30/0)** para mazmorras y raids de 10: Disc Twin Disciplines 5, Silent Resolve 3, Mental Strength 3, Mental Agility 1, Inner Focus, Improved PW:S 3, Meditation 3, Soul Warding, Penance; Holy Improved Renew 3, Holy Specialization 5, Divine Fury 5, Twilight Focus 2, Holy Nova, Inspiration 3, Improved Healing 3, Holy Binding, Litany of Light 2, Spiritual Guidance 5.
  - Soul Warding: sin CD de 4 s en PW:S y −15 % de coste. Improved PW:S 3/3: +20 % de absorción. Improved Healing reduce un 15 % el coste de Penance. Alternar Penance con Greater/Flash Heal activa Litany of Light.
- Legacy a nivel 60: árbol Resourcefulness (Reagent Economy, Permanence, Gourmand; The Quick and the Dead, Reinforce; Diplomat).

## Fiabilidad
- 2026-11-10. El orden del widget empieza en el 10, pero las builds son de nivel 60.
