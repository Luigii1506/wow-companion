# src-method: Ruins of Lordaeron

## Puntos clave
- 15–22, Tirisfal Glades, encima de Undercity; ambas facciones; 6 jefes. Sin coordenadas.
- Horde (6, nivel 16): *The Wrath of Rath'mael* (Deathguard Kristof; empieza con *Rear Guard Patrol*), *A Frightened Request* (Tabitha Heartweaver, Silverpine), *Light's Justice* (Morbin Lightbane, Undercity), *The New Plague* (Theodore Griffs, Undercity; empieza con *A Recipe For Death*), *Unending Torment* (drop de The Baron), *Crest of Lordaeron* (dentro).
- Alliance (4): *Crest of Lordaeron*, *Abominable Creatures*, *Bloodied Insignia*, *Remember That I Love You*.
- Jefes: Witherfang, The Baron (golpes de casi media vida al tanque), Viktor the Vile y The Abandoned (opcionales, por oleadas), Bjork, Rath'mael (Flamestrike).
- Botín de tela: Leftover Abomination Skin (The Baron), Rotmender's Leggings (The Abandoned), Tuskwrap Belt (Bjork), Rotmender's Treads (Rath'mael). Bastones: Segmented Spider Leg, Coldspire Staff. Maza: Scepter of the Abandoned.

## Fiabilidad
- Actualizada el 06-11 (lanzamiento). Sin recompensas de misión ni XP.
