# src-method: Shadowfang Keep

## Puntos clave
- 20–30, Silverpine Forest, sobre todo Horde; vuelo más cercano: The Sepulcher; 20–30 min; 9 jefes (uno raro).
- Horde: *The Book of Ur* (Keeper Bel'dugar, Undercity, nivel 16), *Deathstalkers in Shadowfang* (High Executor Hadrec, Sepulcher), *Arugal Must Die* (Dalar Dawnweaver, Sepulcher). Clase: *A Moon-Kissed Blade*, *The Test of Righteousness*, *The Orb of Soran'ruk*.
- Jefes: Rethilgore, Razorclaw, Baron Silverlaine (−75% sanación al tanque), Commander Springvale, Odo, Deathsworn Captain (raro), Fenrus (veneno), Wolf Master Nandos, Archmage Arugal.
- Botín caster: Sorcerer Collar, Bloody Apron, Blindwatcher's Sight, Odo's Ley Staff, Feline Mantle, Belt of Arugal, Robes of Arugal, Meteor Shard, Worgenbane Talisman.

## Fiabilidad
- Actualizada el 20-10 (beta). Pestañas de misiones mal etiquetadas; sin recompensas ni XP.
