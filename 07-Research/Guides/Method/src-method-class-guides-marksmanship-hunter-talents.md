# Method: Marksmanship Hunter, talentos

## Puntos clave
- Decodificadas del widget con `ForeverTalentsData-1.60.1-2nov.json` (Method) y nombres de ForeverDB (`foreverdb.net/class/<Clase>/talents`).
- **Lone Wolf 5/33/13**: Lethal Attacks 10–14, Careful Aim 15–19, Lone Wolf 20, Hawk Eye 21–23, Rapid Killing 24, Trueshot Aura 25, Rapid Killing 26, Mortal Shots 27–31, Rapid Recuperation 32–33, Barrage 34, Ranged Weapon Spec. 35–39, Sniper Shot 40, Barrage 41–42; luego Deadly Aspects 5, Improved Tracking 5, Entrapment 5, Surefooted 3.
- **Pet Build 16/35/0**: MM igual sin Lone Wolf (Barrage 3 antes, Efficiency 3/5 al final) y BM: Deadly Aspects 5, Focused Fire 2, Imp. Revive Pet 2, Endurance Training 1, Unleashed Fury 5, Bestial Swiftness.
- Lone Wolf, probablemente lo mejor en endgame. Para tenerlo antes, mover un punto de Rapid Killing.
- Improved Tracking solo da +5 % si rastreas el tipo de criatura correcto.
- Trueshot Aura: uno por raid; si no, punto a Efficiency. Legacy: Reagent Economy, Gourmand, Permanence.
