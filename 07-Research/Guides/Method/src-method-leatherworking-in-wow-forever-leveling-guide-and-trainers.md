# Method: Leatherworking en WoW Forever

## Puntos clave
- Rangos: Apprentice 1–75 (nivel 5), Journeyman 75–150 (skill 50, nivel 10), Expert 150–225 (skill 125, nivel 20), Artisan 225–300 (skill 200, nivel 35). Costes: no indicados.
- Ruta solo hasta 225 (225–300 "Work In Progress"). 13 pasos: Light Armor Kit → Cured Light Hide → Embossed Leather Gloves → Cured Medium Hide → Light Leather Pants → Hillman's Shoulders → Heavy Leather → Cured Heavy Hide → Hillman's Leather Gloves → Barbaric Shoulders → Guardian Gloves → Nightscape Headband → Nightscape Pants.
- Lista de compra (totales): 1–75 Light Leather 93, Light Hide 28, Salt 27, Coarse Thread 42; 75–150 Light Leather 150, Medium Leather 245, Medium Hide 15, Heavy Hide 20, Fine Thread 35, Salt 75 (+ curados intermedios); 150–225 Medium Leather 70, Heavy Leather 200, Thick Leather 285, Fine Thread 80, Silken Thread 90 (+ curados). No da materiales por receta.
- Entrenadores Horde cercanos: Shelene Rhobart (Tirisfal 65.4, 60.1, Apprentice), Dan Golthas (Undercity 70.8, 58.6, Apprentice). Journeyman solo en Orgrimmar (Karolek) y Thunder Bluff (Tarn); Expert Una (Thunder Bluff); Artisan Hahrana Ironhide (Feralas 74.4, 43.0).
- Incoherencias internas: rangos de entrenador desplazados (p. ej. Cured Medium Hide a skill 75 "Expert", Nightscape Pants "Master"); Light Leather Pants desbloquea a 70 pero se usa desde 90.
