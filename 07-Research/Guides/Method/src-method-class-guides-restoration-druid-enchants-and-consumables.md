# Method — Restoration Druid: encantamientos y consumibles

## Puntos clave
- Arma 2M Mighty Healing Power; collar Healing Power; hombros Chromatic Mantle of the Dawn; capa Greater Resistance (alt. Subtlety); pecho Major Intellect (alt. Greater Stats); muñecas Healing Power (alt. Superior Intellect); guantes Healing Power; pies Spirit (alt. Greater Stamina); cabeza y piernas TBD.
- Frascos: Flask of Distilled Wisdom o Flask of Natural Swiftness (raid). Elixires baratos: Greater Cleric's Elixir, Elixir of the Sages.
- Pociones (CD compartido): Major Rejuvenation, Major Mana, Major Mender's, Living Action. Bebida Sage's Tea; Brilliant Wizard Oil.
